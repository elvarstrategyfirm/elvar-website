01Private and Professional Communication

All conversations, sessions and materials shared between you and Elvar Strategy Firm are treated as confidential. We do not disclose your information to others unless:

  • You give explicit, written permission to share specific details.
  • We are legally or ethically required to disclose information, as described in the Exceptions section below.

02Athletes Under 18

For athletes under 18, we partner with parents and guardians. Session recaps, weekly actions and notes for parents may be shared with the athlete's parent or guardian, including through the athlete's partner portal.

For team partnerships, we may share general, team-level themes with coaches and program leaders. We do not share an individual athlete's personal details with their team or coaches without permission.

03Storage of Information

Session notes, intake forms and performance materials are stored securely and are only accessible to your consultant. We use secure digital storage and password protection where applicable.

Session recaps in the partner portal are password protected and intended only for the athlete and their family. Please do not share your portal password.

04Not Bound by Therapist-Client Privilege

Although confidentiality is a top priority, Elvar Strategy Firm is not a licensed therapy or medical practice. Your information is therefore not protected by therapist-client or doctor-patient privilege under state or federal law. However, we voluntarily commit to holding your information with discretion and professionalism.

05Exceptions to Confidentiality

We may be legally or ethically required to disclose information if any of the following apply:

  • You disclose an intent to harm yourself or others.
  • There is suspected abuse or neglect of a minor, elderly person or dependent adult.
  • We are compelled by a court order or legal investigation.
Need support right now? In the U.S., you can call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) at any time. In an emergency, call 911.

06Photos and Testimonials

We only use client names, photos or testimonials on our website, social media or marketing materials with permission. For athletes under 18, we ask for a parent or guardian's permission. You can ask us to remove your photo or testimonial at any time.

07Use of Aggregated Insights

From time to time, we may analyze trends across clients to improve our services. This information is always de-identified and aggregated. No individual names or specific situations will ever be shared without consent.

08Communication and Tools

We may communicate by email, phone, text or video. We also use trusted third-party tools to run our services, such as online scheduling, video calls and writing tools that help us prepare session recaps. While we take steps to protect your privacy, no digital platform is 100% secure, and you accept some inherent risk when using electronic communication.

Information collected through the Elvar app is covered by the Elvar App Privacy Policy.

09Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. See also our Terms of Service.
