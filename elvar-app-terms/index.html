04What Elvar Does

Elvar allows athletes to:

  • Submit post-practice and post-game performance reflections

Elvar is a coaching communication and self-reflection tool. It is not a medical, psychological or clinical service and should not be used as one.

Elvar is not monitored around the clock. Coaches may not see a reflection or message right away, so never use the app to ask for urgent help. If you or someone you know is in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), talk to a trusted adult, or call 911 in an emergency.
